UK constituency and local-authority records no longer have `x` or `y` fields in the contract or in the gateway's OpenAPI document. A record that carries either, even as `null`, is now refused, so a result from an app deployed for an older policyengine.py version that still sends them fails validation. The contract's pydantic requirement returns to 2.0 or later, since nothing uses field-level `exclude_if` any more.
