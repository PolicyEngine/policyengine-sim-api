A GET request, such as a job or budget-window status read, that Modal answers with its own `modal-http: internal error` 500 because the gateway's container stopped mid-request (as when Modal preempts it) is now sent again by the Simulation Entrypoint: up to twice, after waits of 0.5 and 1 second, with no attempt starting more than 5 seconds after the read began, and the whole read ending within the entrypoint's 25-second gateway read timeout. The first answer from the gateway itself is relayed, so the request is no longer recorded as an error, and each read sent again is recorded as a WARNING event, `simulation_entry_backend_read_retried`, with its job or batch ID and attempt counts. Only when Modal answers every attempt does the caller get a 503 with `Retry-After` instead of that 500, so pollers keep waiting instead of reporting a failed job. A failed job's 500 from the gateway, and a submission's 500, are relayed unchanged and never sent again.
