A GET request, such as a job or budget-window status read, that Modal answers with its own `modal-http: internal error` 500 because the gateway's container stopped mid-request (as when Modal preempts it) now gets a 503 with `Retry-After` from the Simulation Entrypoint instead of that 500, so pollers keep waiting instead of reporting a failed job. A failed job's 500 from the gateway, and a submission's 500, are unchanged.
