Each call of the v2 app's simulation workers, `SimulationWorkerUS`, `SimulationWorkerUK` and `SimulationSegmentWorkerUS`, and of the plain functions kept for callers on older code, now carries `container_start` (`restored_from_snapshot`, `took_snapshot` or `no_snapshot`) and `container_call_number` on its operation log record and span, as the run_simulation app's workers already do, so one query shows both apps' restores. Each such call's operation also names the v2 app in `modal_app_name`.
