Current-law US national baselines of comparison-path reports in the v2 report app (shadow comparisons with production jobs, and the operator-only `POST /internal/stage12/reports`) load their region-group segments from the precomputed baselines again. Since the precomputed baselines were planned as simulation runs store them, the comparison-path coordinator still gave its baseline the report's plan, so every segment computed a cache key the precompute never stores and calculated its baseline. It now gives the baseline the baseline's stored plan whenever that plan holds every column the report reads from it, as a report run's coordinator does; a report with `include_cliffs: true` and every reform keep the report's plan. A comparison report whose reform turns a labour-supply response on now splits its current-law baseline into 20 segments instead of calculating it in one container.
