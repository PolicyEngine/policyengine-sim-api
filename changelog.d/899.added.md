Added the `simulation.simulations` and `simulation.reports` tables, in migration revision `0002` of the `simulation` schema. A simulation records what to run independently of model versions: its kind, country, policy, dynamic, and either a household or a region code with an optional dataset name. A report records which baseline and reform simulations it compares, its year and its options. Both refer to the Public API's policies, households and dynamics with `ON DELETE RESTRICT` foreign keys and keep a unique v1 ID. Nothing writes either table yet.
