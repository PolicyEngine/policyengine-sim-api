Stage 12 now calculates UK-wide and nation reports on the release's local-area dataset (the dataset UK constituency and local-authority regions use): the UK worker loads that dataset, and such a report's run returns its full results together with its constituency and local-authority results and the codes of the areas disclosure control withheld, which it stores as `withheld_area_codes` rows. The economy output gains optional `constituency_withheld_codes` and `local_authority_withheld_codes`, left out of every other result. The v2 version manifest's UK entry declares the local-area dataset (`local_area_reports`), under a new key that readers released before it never read.
