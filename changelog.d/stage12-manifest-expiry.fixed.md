A deployment no longer treats a Stage 12 v2 manifest that Modal expired as an empty one. A scheduled workflow reads every key of the manifest once a day in `staging` and `main`, so Modal's expiry of a Dict entry after 7 days without a read or write no longer removes it, and fails when it is missing. A deployment's worker publication now refuses a missing manifest before its checks, instead of making its unchecked worker the default and leaving out every earlier worker; an operator publishes the first worker of a new or lost manifest.
